*A robot needs 4 insertions; all must succeed. Dusty = 3 mm camera error, worn = motors at 25% torque, tight = 0.1 mm clearance, harsh = all three. Measured under Mars gravity. Teleop delays are one-way: the Moon is 1.3 s away and Mars 240–1,440 s, so even the Moon is too far for precise teleoperation. Supervisory control (send goals, let the robot execute) works from Earth.

JavaScript port of the Python model in the VN-Mach repository (same rules and numbers). Prices, launch costs and contract demand are assumptions; change them in vnmach/body.py.
